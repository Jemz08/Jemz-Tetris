HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Arrange falling blocks into complete horizontal lines. A full line disappears and scores points. The game ends when blocks stack to the top.

SCORING

1 line = 100 × level · 2 lines = 300 × level · 3 lines = 500 × level · TETRIS (4 lines) = 800 × level. Soft drop adds 1 point per cell, hard drop adds 2 per cell. Every 10 lines raises your level and speeds up the game.

CONTROLS

HOLD stores a piece for later. The ghost outline shows where the piece will land. Multiplayer: the last player standing wins — beaten players are ranked by when they topped out, ties broken by score.

ONLINE (2 PLAYERS)

Choose 2 PLAYERS → TWO DEVICES · ONLINE. One device creates a room, the other joins with the code. No account needed — rooms connect directly via WebRTC. Both players race; the first to top out loses.
